Power sequence

Applies to every configuration. The cell and the 24 V supply are both sources.

The one rule
24 V goes on first and off last. The cell goes on last and off first.
The Arduino's 5 V pin is the board's 5 V rail — plugging in the Arduino is switching on 5 V.

Power up

#ActionNotes
1Seat both supply leads with the output offBlack to J3 first, then red to J2. No live plugging.

4Power on both 617 electrometersAny time before the script. Allow them to warm up.
5Power on the EDFA100PInterlock shorted. Output terminated or already aimed at the cell.

Why the order matters
With 5 V up, K1 energised and 24 V absent, K1 shorts out R1 and the cell drives straight into the DigiPot's input clamp — about 80% of its ±20 mA absolute maximum. That is the case that destroys a chip. D4 sits between the two rails and does not protect the PV input path.
Laser
The EDFA100P is Class 3B at 1550 nm, which is invisible. It emits up to 30 mW of amplified spontaneous emission with no optical input, at any pump current, whenever it is enabled. Terminate the output before running. Never look into the fibre bulkhead. If the interlock trips, it was opened by a person — software will not re-enable it and neither should you until you know why.

Shortcut for repeat runs: the jacks do not have to be cycled each time. A dark cell sources almost nothing, so blocking the light is equivalent to unplugging it and spares the connectors.

Wiring 1  ·  Handheld meter + 24 V

Board bring-up. No cell, no laser, no bench meters. The handheld stands in for the cell.

Both the supply return and the meter's COM lead land on J3. Use a stacking banana plug.

Do not put the red lead in the meter's 10A socket. In that socket the meter is a piece of wire and you would be shorting J1 to J3.

Software

EDFA_ENABLED = false  ·  DMM_ENABLED = false  ·  RUN = "verify" to check the board, or "ramp" to watch the whole range.

Power order for this setup

Up: 24 V output on → Arduino USB in → run.   Down: stop → Arduino USB out → 24 V output off.
The meter leads stay plugged in throughout, both ways. In ohms mode a handheld is not a source and cannot hurt anything.

Wiring 2  ·  Two Keithley 617 + 24 V

Proving the GPIB chain and the 617 command dialect before a cell is involved. Meter inputs left open.

GPIB is a bus. One adapter serves both meters — IEEE-488 connectors stack, so the second cable runs meter to meter rather than back to the PC.

Before this works

  • Give each 617 its own primary address from its front panel. They default to 27, which collides. The file expects 22 for volts and 23 for amps — change the meters or change the file.
  • Confirm both appear: visadevlist. With no instruments attached it raises unableToFindResourcesDefaultTimeout, which is the healthy empty-bench answer. “Unable to find VISA installations” is the broken one and means NI-VISA is missing.
  • DMM_ENABLED = true, RUN = "meters".

What it proves

Both meters answer U0 with a status word starting 617, accept their setup string, return a clean U1 error word, and deliver ten readings. That covers the adapter, the cable, the addresses and the command dialect — everything except the measurement wiring, which Wiring 3 adds.

Absolute maximum on the 617 volts input is 250 V peak. The amps function stops at 20 mA and there is nothing above it; a cell that outruns 20 mA needs an external shunt.

Wiring 3  ·  Full experiment

PV cell, both 617s, EDFA100P, 24 V supply, board. This is RUN = "sweep".

3a   The measurement loop

Thick lines carry the cell current. The ammeter breaks the PV+ lead outside the board; the voltmeter taps the cell terminals directly, upstream of the ammeter.

3b   Cables back to the PC

Three independent chains. GPIB is a bus, so both 617s hang off one adapter with the second cable running meter to meter.

The voltmeter goes on the cell side of the ammeter — directly on the cell leads, not across J1 and J3. Downstream of the ammeter it reads low by the burden drop. On a 617 that is under 1 mV on every range except 20 mA, where it is 3 mV, so it no longer swamps the Isc end — but the rule is unchanged.

Software

RUN = "sweep"  ·  EDFA_ENABLED = true  ·  DMM_ENABLED = true  ·  SERIAL_PORT, EDFA_PORT, DMM_V_ADDRESS, DMM_I_ADDRESS all set.

Enter the cell's real numbers in ISC_FULL, VOC_FULL and POWER_FULL — they size the ammeter range and print the estimate. An ISC_FULL above 20 mA is refused, because the 617 has no range above it.

For LEVEL_MODE = "power" the calibration arrays CAL_CURRENT_MA and CAL_POWER_MW must be filled from a measured pump-current-to-optical-power sweep. Outside their measured range the script refuses rather than extrapolating.

Output

pvload_<stamp>.csv for per-point readings and pvload_<stamp>_levels.csv for what is constant across a level, appended one level at a time so an abort keeps every completed level. Nothing is ever overwritten.
